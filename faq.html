Time-Remapped anime clips are scenes where the playback speed is altered within the clip itself, shifting between slow-mo and fast motion instead of staying at one flat speed. Editors use this to control pacing for more of a dramatic effect, & is often synced to the beat of the music.

My workflow is to manually download anime episodes, skim for clips/audio, then render, compress & upload. For time-remapped clips, I run them through NodeVideo afterwards & the only AI involved is optical flow interpolation. Overall it's a pretty time-consuming process.

Both Normal & Time-Remapped Clips are encoded with H.264 - MP4 at the anime's native fps & resolution; usually 1080p/24fps. Audios will be MP3 @ 320 kbps.
