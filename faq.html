I manually download anime episodes, skim for clips/audios, then render. For any Twixtor clips, I run them through NodeVideo afterwards & then finally compress & upload for both.

Both Normal & Time-Remapped Clips are encoded with H.264 - MP4 at the anime's native fps & resolution; usually 1080p/24fps. Audios will be MP3 @ 320 kbps.

Twixtor'd clips are footage that's been sped up or slowed down using time-remapping & instead of just stretching or holding frames (which looks choppy), the motion is filled in with in-between frames using interpolation to keep it looking buttery smooth.
